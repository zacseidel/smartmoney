About

Full House Resorts Inc owns, operates, develops, manages, and invests in casinos and related hospitality and entertainment facilities. It offers facilities related to gaming, hotel, dining, entertainment, retail, and other amenities. The group's reportable segments are Midwest & South, West, and Contracted Sports Wagering, It generates the majority of its revenue from the Midwest & South segment which includes Silver Slipper Casino and Hotel, Rising Star Casino Resort, and American Place.
