About

Fluor is one of the largest global providers of engineering, procurement, construction, fabrication, operations, and maintenance services. It serves a wide range of end markets, including oil and gas, chemicals, mining, metals, and transportation. The company's business is organized into three core segments: urban solutions, mission solutions, and energy solutions. Fluor generated $15.5 billion in revenue in 2025.
