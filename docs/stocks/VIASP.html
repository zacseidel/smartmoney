About

Via Renewables Inc is an independent retail energy services company. The company provides residential and commercial customers in competitive U.S. markets with electricity and natural gas sourced from wholesale providers, offering an alternative to traditional utilities. It bills customers based on consumption at fixed or variable prices, while delivery is handled by local regulated utility companies through existing infrastructure.
