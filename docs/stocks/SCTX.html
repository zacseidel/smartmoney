About

Scribe Therapeutics Inc is a clinical-stage biotechnology company engaged in the development of in vivo CRISPR-based genetic medicines. Its research and development activities focus on cardiovascular and metabolic diseases, including atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD).
