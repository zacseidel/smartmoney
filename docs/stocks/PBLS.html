About

Parabilis Medicines Inc s a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company. It is focused on the development of medicines targeting protein interactions associated with human diseases. It uses a proprietary platform to develop stabilized helical peptide therapeutics, referred to as Helicons, designed to bind and modulate specific proteins.
