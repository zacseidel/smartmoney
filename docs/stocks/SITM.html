About

SiTime Corp is a provider of Precision Timing solutions to the international electronics industry, providing the timing functionality needed for electronics to operate reliably and correctly. The Company's products are designed to address a wide range of applications across a broad array of end markets. The Company operates a fabless business model and leverages its international network of distributors to address the end markets it serves.
