About

Glacier Bancorp Inc is a regional bank holding company providing commercial banking services through its wholly owned bank subsidiary, Glacier Bank. The group provides a full range of banking services to individuals, small to medium-sized businesses, community organizations, and public entities from several locations in Montana, Idaho, Utah, Washington, Wyoming, Colorado, Arizona, Nevada, and Texas.
