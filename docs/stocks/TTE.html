About

TotalEnergies is an integrated oil and gas company that explores for, produces, and refines oil around the world. In 2025, it produced 1.5 million barrels of liquids and 5.4 billion cubic feet of natural gas per day. At the end of 2025, reserves stood at 11.2 billion barrels of oil equivalent, 55% of which are liquids. During 2025, it had LNG sales of 43.9 million metric tons. The company owns interests in refineries with a capacity of nearly 1.8 million barrels a day, primarily in Europe, distributes refined products in 65 countries, and manufactures commodity and specialty chemicals.
