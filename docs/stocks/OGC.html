About

OceanaGold Corp is a growing intermediate gold and copper producer committed to safely and responsibly maximizing the generation of Free Cash Flow from its operations and delivering robust returns for its shareholders. It has a portfolio of four operating mines: the Haile Gold Mine in the United States of America; the Didipio Mine in the Philippines; and the Macraes and Waihi operations in New Zealand. The company generates key revenue from Haile mines. · website
