About

Chord Energy Corp is an independent exploration and production (E&P) company engaged in the acquisition, exploration, development and production of crude oil, NGL and natural gas prominently in the Williston Basin, with limited non-operated interests in the Marcellus Shale. It operates in one reportable business segment, which is the E&P of crude oil, NGL and natural gas. The company derives revenue from the sale of crude oil, NGL and natural gas production.
