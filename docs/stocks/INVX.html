About

Innovex International Inc designs, manufactures, sells, and rents mission-critical engineered products to the oil and natural gas industry. Its products are Subsea, Surface Wellheads, Well Construction, Well Completion, Production Solutions, Fishing & Intervention. Its products support upstream onshore and offshore activities.
