About

Genworth Financial Inc is a diversified insurance holding company that provides various mortgage and life insurance products. The company has four main operating business segments: Enact, Closed Block segment, and Corporate and Other. The company's product portfolio includes various financial products such as traditional life insurance, mortgage insurance, fixed annuities, and variable annuities. The majority of the revenue is generated by the Enact segment. The company earns the maximum of its revenue in the United States. LIFE INSURANCE · website
