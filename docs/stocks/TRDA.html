About

Entrada Therapeutics Inc is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company. It aims to transform the lives of patients by establishing a new class of medicines that engage intracellular targets that have long been considered inaccessible. The company's Endosomal Escape Vehicle (EEV)-therapeutics are designed to enable the efficient intracellular delivery into a variety of organs and tissues, resulting in an improved therapeutic index and protein-based programs for the potential treatment of neuromuscular and ocular diseases, among others.
