About

Idacorp is a holding company for Idaho Power. Idacorp is a vertically integrated utility that generates, transmits, and distributes electricity to 670,000 customers in southern Idaho and eastern Oregon. The utility owns 1.8 gigawatts of hydro generation, 400 MW of coal generation, 1.4 GW of natural gas generation, and 300 MW of energy storage. ELECTRIC SERVICES · website
