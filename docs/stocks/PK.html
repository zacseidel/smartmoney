About

Park Hotels & Resorts owns upper-upscale and luxury hotels, with 21,042 rooms across 33 hotels in the United States. Park also has interests through joint ventures in another 1,712 rooms in two US hotels. Park was spun out of Hilton Worldwide Holdings at the start of 2017, so most of its hotels are still under the Hilton brand. The company has sold all its international hotels and many of its lower-quality US hotels to focus on high-quality assets in domestic gateway markets. HOTELS & MOTELS · website
