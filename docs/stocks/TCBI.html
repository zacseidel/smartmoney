About

Texas Capital Bancshares Inc is a registered bank holding company and a full-service financial services firm that delivers customised solutions to businesses, entrepreneurs and individual customers. It is a secured lender with the majority of the loans held for investment, excluding mortgage finance loans and other national lines of business. Texas Capital has established commercial banking, consumer banking, investment banking and wealth management capabilities. STATE COMMERCIAL BANKS · website
