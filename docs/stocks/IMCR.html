About

Immunocore Holdings PLC is a commercial-stage biotechnology company. Using its ImmTAX platform, the company is developing a deep pipeline in multiple therapeutic areas, including clinical stage programs in oncology and infectious disease, pre-clinical programs in autoimmune disease, and earlier pre-clinical programs across three therapeutic areas. The company's main product, KIMMTRAK, is used for the treatment of unresectable or metastatic uveal melanoma (mUM).
