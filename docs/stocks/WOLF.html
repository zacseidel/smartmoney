About

Wolfspeed Inc develops and manufactures semiconductor technologies based on silicon carbide (SiC) for power electronics applications. Its product portfolio includes SiC MOSFETs, Schottky diodes, and power modules used in power electronics applications. The company also provides reference designs, evaluation kits, and gate driver boards to support the development and integration of its SiC products across industries such as electric vehicles, renewable energy, and industrial systems.
