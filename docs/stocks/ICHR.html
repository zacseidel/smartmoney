About

Ichor Holdings Ltd designs, engineers, and manufactures critical fluid delivery subsystems and components mainly for semiconductor capital equipment, along with solutions for defense/aerospace and medical industries. Its product portfolio includes gas and chemical delivery subsystems that enable precise delivery, monitoring, and control of specialized gases and reactive liquid chemistries used in semiconductor manufacturing processes such as etch, electroplating, and cleaning.
