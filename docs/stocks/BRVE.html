About

Braveheart Bio Inc is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on developing therapeutics to treat hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM) and other cardiovascular diseases. Its flagship product candidate, BHB-1893, is an oral small-molecule cardiac myosin inhibitor being developed for the treatment of obstructive HCM (oHCM) and non-obstructive HCM (nHCM).
