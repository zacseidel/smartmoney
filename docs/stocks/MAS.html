About

Masco is a global manufacturer of branded home improvement products, with a focus on plumbing fixtures (70% of revenue) and decorative architectural coatings (30% of revenue). The company primarily serves the repair and remodel market, with products such as faucets, showerheads, and paint sold through retail, wholesale, and e-commerce channels.
