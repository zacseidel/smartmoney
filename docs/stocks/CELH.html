About

Celsius Holdings operates in the energy drink subsegment of the global nonalcoholic beverage market, with 95% of revenue concentrated in North America. It owns three energy drink brands: Celsius, Alani Nu, and Rockstar Energy. It focuses on product innovation and marketing while outsourcing manufacturing and packaging to third-party co-packers and distribution to PepsiCo.
