About

Aris Mining Corp is a Canadian gold mining company focused on South America. It is mainly engaged in the acquisition, exploration, development, and operation of gold properties in Colombia and Guyana. Aris Mining operates the Segovia and Marmato Mines and the Soto Norte Project in Colombia. Additionally, it owns the Toroparu Project in Guyana. The company considers its Segovia and Marmato Mines in Colombia, its Toroparu Project in Guyana, its Soto Norte Project in Colombia, and its corporate functions in Canada and other corporate entities as its reportable segments.
