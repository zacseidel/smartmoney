About

TTM Technologies Inc manufactures technology products, including mission systems, RF components, RF/microwave assemblies, and advanced interconnect products such as PCBs and substrates. It operates through three segments: A&D, Commercial, and RF&S Components. The A&D segment supports aerospace and defense missions with PCBs, assemblies, microelectronics, and integrated systems. The Commercial segment, generating the majority of revenue, serves automotive, medical, industrial, and data center markets. The RF&S Components segment provides TTM-designed RF components and COTS products. The Company PRINTED CIRCUIT BOARDS · website
