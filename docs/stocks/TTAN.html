About

ServiceTitan Inc is an end-to-end technology platform built for contractors to transform the performance of their businesses. The platform provides business owners, technicians, customer service representatives and other key office staff with technology tools designed to help customers grow revenue, drive operational efficiencies, deliver a superior end-customer experience and monitor key business drivers in real-time. The Company also has subsidiaries in Australia, Armenia and British Columbia, Canada that serve as research and development and support centers. SERVICES-PREPACKAGED SOFTWARE · website
