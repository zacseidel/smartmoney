About

NNN REIT Inc is a real estate investment trust that acquires, owns, invests in, and develops high-quality properties mainly leased under long-term net leases with minimal capital expenditure requirements. The company focuses mainly on retail properties, with a portfolio including convenience stores, automotive service properties, restaurants, theatres, entertainment venues, dealerships, and other retail-related assets.
