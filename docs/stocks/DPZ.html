About

Domino's is the world's largest pizza chain, surpassing $20 billion in system sales with over 22,100 stores across more than 90 markets at the end of 2025. Around 33% of its units are in the US. The business is 99% franchised. Domino's operates through three segments: supply chain (60% of revenue), US stores (33%), and international franchises (7%). The firm generates the bulk of its revenue by supplying food to stores in the US and Canada through a vertically integrated network of 25 manufacturing and supply chain facilities, which primarily produce and distribute dough, and collecting brand WHOLESALE-GROCERIES & RELATED PRODUCTS · website
