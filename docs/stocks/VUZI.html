About

Vuzix Corp is engaged in the design, manufacture, and marketing of Artificial Intelligence (AI)-powered Smart Glasses, Waveguides, and Augmented Reality (AR) technologies. Its solutions serve the enterprise, medical, defense, security, and select consumer markets, offering cutting-edge wearable computing and display technologies that enhance productivity and operational efficiency. Its products include near-eye displays (NEDs), heads-up displays (HUDs), and wearable computing devices and third-party original design manufacturer (ODM) and original equipment manufacturer (OEM) devices.
