About

The Magnum Ice Cream Co NV is a spinoff from Unilever, pure-play in manufacturing and selling a broad portfolio of ice-cream brands. TMICC ice-cream portfolio includes Magnum, Ben and Jerrys, Cornetto and the Heart brand. The company is organised into three geographic regions (i) Europe and ANZ, (ii) Americas, and (iii) AMEA. · website
