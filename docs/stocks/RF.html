About

Regions Financial is one of the midsized regional banks in the US, with around $160 billion in total assets as of the end of first quarter of 2026. Headquartered in Birmingham, Alabama, Regions Financial has a footprint mostly in the US Southeast. The bank provides a diversified set of financial services in retail banking, commercial banking, card and treasury management, wealth management, and capital markets.
