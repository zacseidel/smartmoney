About

Doximity Inc provides an online platform, which enables physicians and other healthcare professionals to collaborate with colleagues, stay up to date with the latest medical news and research, manage their careers and on-call schedules, streamline documentation and administrative paperwork, and conduct virtual patient visits. The Company's customers include pharmaceutical companies and health systems that connect with healthcare professionals through the Company's digital Marketing, Hiring, and Workflow Solutions.
