About

Banzai International Inc is a Software as a Service (SaaS) company operating in the marketing technology (MarTech) industry. The group provides customers with tools to help them market and sell with greater efficiency and impact. Its customers include Amazon, Dell, Salesforce, Aflac, Thermo Fisher Scientific, RBC Wealth Management, Fitch Group, and many other brands. The Company has three reportable operating segments: Banzai Operating Co, Inc., OpenReel, and Vidello.
